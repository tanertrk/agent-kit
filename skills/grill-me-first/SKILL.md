# Grill Me First

Goal: sharpen the brief before any work starts, so the result is right the first time.

## Process
1. **Restate the goal in one sentence** and ask whether it's correct.
2. **Ask questions in rounds**, at most 3-4 per round, the most important first. For each question, **suggest a recommended answer** so the manager can just confirm ("Recommendation: X, OK?").
3. Ask about these areas (only what's actually missing):
   - Goal & success: what counts as a good result?
   - Audience: who reads or decides (team, the regional manager, CEO)?
   - Facts & figures: what data is available?
   - Constraints: budget, staff, time, company rules
   - Risks & objections: what could management or the team push back on?
   - Format & language: document, message, table; DE/EN/TR
4. **Challenge weak points** openly and factually: gaps in the argument, unrealistic assumptions, missing numbers.
5. Stop once the brief is clear (usually 2-3 rounds). Then a **short summary of the brief** -> after confirmation, start the work.

## Rules
- No final output before the brief is confirmed.
- Don't ask about things that are already known.
- Tone: direct, constructive, never condescending.
